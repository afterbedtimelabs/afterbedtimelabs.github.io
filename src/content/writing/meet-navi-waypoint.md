**A little company at the computer, and a helping hand when the adventure moves into Minecraft.**

Navi and Waypoint explore different sides of a personal AI companion. Navi is the desktop project, previously introduced on this site as Riku. Waypoint is a separate Minecraft companion; they aren’t two names for the same application.

Navi’s current code brings together a desktop character, text conversation, remembered preferences, and optional local voice. The interesting challenge is making those pieces feel comfortable together: a useful reply, an expression that fits, and a companion that can stay quietly nearby.

Waypoint takes that idea into a game. Its implemented controllers handle actions such as following, gathering resources, and crafting supported items. Those actions have specific rules; it isn’t a promise that an AI can do anything you ask.

I like the mix of personality and practical help. Small, dependable interactions are the part worth getting right.

**Work in progress:** both are private prototypes. Their code was inspected for this introduction, but a live conversation and Minecraft session were not verified during this review.
