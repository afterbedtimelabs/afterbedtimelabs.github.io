**A tiny truck, three loose cartons, and a delivery waiting at the other end of the mountain road.**

NightStock started as a physics experiment. Its current first shift takes that small kei truck into rural Japan after dark: headlights on the asphalt, a red bridge over the ravine, cedar trees crowding the road, and a tunnel on the way down to the valley.

Before leaving, read dispatch on the flip phone, climb out, open the tailgate, and load the cartons yourself. They are loose physical objects, so a sharp turn or sudden stop can rearrange the load. You can drive from the cab or switch to the chase camera. A local radio station keeps you company, with subtitles for its broadcasts.

The destination is YORUMICHI, a convenience store still lit in the small hours. Parking is only the beginning: carry the boxes from the truck to the receiving mat and wait for the handover. The appeal is as much the journey between stops as the delivery itself—keeping a modest truck and its unruly load together on a quiet road.

**Work in progress:** V1.3.1 includes a playable first delivery shift in a local Windows build. Driving feel, cargo handling, startup, and consistency are still being refined. There is no public download yet.
