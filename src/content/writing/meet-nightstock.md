**A tiny truck, a wobbly load, and the eternal optimism of taking that corner a little too fast.**

NightStock is my physics-driven kei truck experiment. You start with eight loose cardboard boxes in the back and a test course full of straights, bumps, hills, and turns. The boxes are actual moving objects: braking and steering can shift the load, and a rough drive can spill it.

You can switch between a chase camera and the cab, open the tailgate, climb out, and pick boxes up again. That little loop—drive, make a mess, recover the cargo—is the part I’m exploring.

It’s fun to build because the truck and its load keep surprising me. A change in grip or suspension can turn the same stretch of road into a very different trip.

**Work in progress:** this is an early physics playground with simple test-course scenery. It isn’t a finished delivery game, and there’s no public download yet.

These in-engine screenshots use the project’s existing scripted camera review. The prototype’s standard driving HUD remains visible; the tuning and help panels are closed.
