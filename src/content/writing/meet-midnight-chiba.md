**A quiet walk home, with just enough wrongness to make the next streetlight look inviting.**

Midnight Chiba is my atmospheric exploration game set in a Japanese neighborhood after dark. You leave the station, wander through residential lanes, pass a shrine and cemetery, and head toward the last warm porch. Walking, looking, and listening are the heart of it.

The fun is in the contrast: a glowing convenience store feels reassuring after a dark stretch of road, while a narrow lane can make an ordinary house feel unfamiliar. There’s a flashlight, optional coffee and a shrine bell, plus supernatural changes along the route.

I like building this because tiny choices in light, sound, and distance can change how a place feels. A corner doesn’t need a big event to make you hesitate.

**Work in progress:** the current prototype has a complete walking route, intended for roughly 10–20 minutes of exploration. Atmosphere and presentation are still being refined; there’s no public download yet.

These are fresh in-engine captures from the existing camera-review script, using the real player’s viewpoint. They aren’t concept art.
