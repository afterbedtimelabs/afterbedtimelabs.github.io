**Your backpack is secretly a deck of cards, and the dungeon would like a word.**

Dungeon Deckbuilder is a fantasy roguelike deckbuilding game built around one simple idea: your equipment supplies your combat cards. Finding new gear can change how you play, while upgrading a favorite piece opens up stronger possibilities.

The current demo has six playable classes, from a sturdy Knight to a Summoner with an elemental companion. Enemies show their next attack, so each turn becomes a small puzzle: defend, strike, or set something up for later? The Mage can combine fire, frost, and lightning, while the Summoner builds a bond with an Avatar.

I enjoy building the moments when a few ordinary choices suddenly fit together. Getting a new item should make you curious about the next fight.

**Work in progress:** there’s a short five-battle demo with rewards, an event, and a rest-or-train stop. It’s still being playtested for balance and readability, with no public play link here yet.

These screenshots come from the running game’s existing UI capture routine. Combat views show opening hands, with no extra cards added for the pictures.
