**A little company at the computer, even when everyone else has gone to bed.**

Riku is my desktop AI companion: a character who can hang out nearby, respond to text, and use optional local voice. The project also has memory for things I explicitly ask it to remember, so the interesting part isn’t just getting an answer—it’s giving those little interactions some continuity.

I like building the space between useful and personable. A reply needs to feel right, an expression needs to fit the moment, and the companion needs to be comfortable sitting quietly alongside whatever I’m doing. Those small details are where this project gets its personality.

**Work in progress:** the desktop, conversation, memory, and voice pieces are implemented, while Riku’s visual identity and everyday feel are still evolving. Character screenshots will come later; the project card keeps the lab’s abstract illustration for now.

You may still see **Navi** in technical screens and the [Gmail Monitor information](/navi/) and [privacy policy](/navi/privacy/). That’s the internal name. **Waypoint** is a separate Minecraft companion, with its own story for another day.
